# Code Of Conduct

Lattice should be a kind, direct, and productive open source space.

Participants are expected to:

- Be respectful of different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Give constructive technical feedback.
- Assume good intent while still naming risks clearly.
- Avoid harassment, insults, personal attacks, and exclusionary behavior.

Unacceptable behavior can be reported through the security or maintainer contact paths documented in this repository. Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation when needed to protect the project.
